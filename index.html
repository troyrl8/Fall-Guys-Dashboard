What stands out?

Observations from the historical Steam data

Free-to-play period

Average concurrent Steam players rose from 6,378 in May 2022 to 17,472 in June and 29,801 in July. This is a timing association, not proof of causation.

Important platform context

Fall Guys moved to free-to-play in June 2022 and stopped accepting new Steam purchases. Steam activity therefore cannot represent the full cross-platform audience.

Launch versus recent activity

The August 2020 launch month had the highest monthly average in this dataset. Later Steam counts reflect a different distribution environment.

Data notes & methodology

Source: Steam Charts — Fall Guys (App 1097150). Monthly average concurrent players and monthly peak concurrent players are transcribed from the published table, accessed November 2026. The last complete month included is October 2026; rolling “last 30 days” is excluded.

Definitions: Monthly average is the average number of players simultaneously online on Steam, not unique players, total downloads, or monthly active users. Monthly change is computed from consecutive monthly averages. Yearly figures average the available monthly averages (not weighted by days).

Interpretation: Steam does not include console or Epic Games Store players. Fall Guys' June 2022 free-to-play transition changed platform availability, so apparent changes in Steam activity should not be interpreted as total game growth or decline. This dashboard is an independent portfolio exercise, not affiliated with Epic Games or Steam Charts.
